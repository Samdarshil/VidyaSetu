THE PROBLEM & THE FIX

Talent and demand rarely speak the same language.

Institutions teach at scale. Industry hires for specific, evolving capability. Between them sits a gap that resumes and grade sheets can't close.

THE PROBLEM

Skill claims without evidence

Resumes list "Python — Advanced" with no way to verify it. Institutions can't see real placement readiness. Industry can't see which colleges actually produce job-ready talent in a given skill.

THE VIDYASETU SOLUTION

Evidence-driven skill intelligence

Every skill is backed by assessments, projects, certifications and industry feedback — giving students, faculty, institutions and industry one shared, trustworthy signal.
